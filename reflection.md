# Mission 3 Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most is Google Cloud Platform because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. I learned that GCP provides useful tools for developing and deploying modern applications, especially for organizations working with data and AI.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and security. They also allow organizations to scale their resources based on their needs. Although their services are similar, each provider has different strengths and tools that can make it more suitable for specific organizations.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. A company should consider its budget, existing technologies, workload, security requirements, scalability, and technical goals before choosing a provider. For example, Azure is a strong choice for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies, while GCP can be a good choice for AI and Machine Learning workloads.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The best choice depends on the specific needs and goals of the client. I also learned how to compare equivalent services and recommend cloud solutions based on different business scenarios.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has improved because it now contains organized research, comparison tables, client recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and a reflection. This mission helped me understand how cloud platforms are used in real-world business situations and improved my ability to document technical work professionally.
